1. Who is responsible

The EventLead website is published by FAYaFATech, {{ CONTACT_ADDRESS }}. For any question about your data, write to {{ CONTACT_EMAIL }} or on WhatsApp at {{ CONTACT_PHONE }}.

2. The data we keep

  • Your account: first name, last name, email address, phone number and password (stored encrypted, we never see it). With Google sign-in, we only receive your name and email address.
  • Guests of an event, added by the organizer: name, phone or email, answer, number of companions, chosen gift, table, arrival time on the day.
  • Payments and contributions: payment method, amount, transaction reference and masked number (for example +509 **** 4455). The full card number never reaches us: it is entered on the payment provider's page.
  • Album photos sent by guests. We remove the GPS location from each photo before saving it.
  • Your messages: contact form, help requests and reviews.
  • Technical data: IP address, used to block repeated submissions and in the site's error log.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the service: send invitations, reminders and thank-you messages, record answers, reserve gifts, prepare the seating plan and the check-in at the door.
  • To collect payment for tickets, VIP Organizer access and contributions, and keep a record of each payment.
  • To answer your messages and help requests.
  • To protect the site against abuse and fix errors.

We do not sell your data and we do not do targeted advertising. Partner ads shown on the site receive no personal data: they only see the number of views and clicks.

4. Who can see it

  • The organizer of an event sees the guests, answers, gifts and contributions of that event.
  • The EventLead team, to run the site and answer help requests.
  • The providers the site needs, each for its own part: the site's host, MonCash, NatCash, Stripe (card) and PayPal for payments, Google for Google sign-in and fonts, OpenStreetMap for the venue map, jsDelivr for display files, WhatsApp and the email service for sending invitations.

Some of these providers are located outside Haiti, in particular in the United States.

5. Cookies

The site only uses the cookies it needs to work: the session (to stay signed in), form protection and your chosen language (kept for one year). There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

If you arrive through one of our campaign links (an address that contains "utm_"), the name of that link is kept in your session. If it leads to a sign-up, a payment, a help request or a message, the team can see which link it came from. This information stays on EventLead and is not shared with anyone.

Your dark mode choice and the closing of the cookie notice are kept only in your browser.

6. How long

  • Your account is kept as long as it exists. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time.
  • The guests, answers, gifts and photos of an event are kept as long as the organizer keeps the event. Deleting the event deletes them too.
  • Payments are kept as accounting records, even after an account is deleted.
  • Site backups keep a copy of the data for a few weeks, then they are replaced.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see your data, have it corrected or deleted, receive a copy, or object to its use. Write to {{ CONTACT_EMAIL }} with your name and the email address or phone number used on the site. We answer within one month.

If you are a guest at an event, you can also contact the organizer who added you.

If you live in the European Union, you can also contact your country's data protection authority.

8. Security

The site runs over HTTPS. Passwords are encrypted. Each guest's personal link cannot be guessed, and neither can the addresses of album photos.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes too. For an important change, we notify organizers by email.
